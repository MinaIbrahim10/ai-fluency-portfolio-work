# Run 3 — Professional Outreach Email

## Input

Audience:
A technical hiring manager at an AI or software company.

Goal:
Write a short professional email introducing my background and asking whether there may be a suitable AI engineering opportunity.

Format:
Professional email.

Constraints:
- Maximum 170 words.
- Professional and direct.
- No exaggerated claims.
- Do not sound desperate or generic.
- Mention Generative AI, LLMs, agentic AI, backend engineering, and TensorFlow open-source work.
- Mention my portfolio.
- End with a polite invitation to discuss relevant opportunities.
- Do not invent company-specific information.

Input facts:
- I work across Generative AI, LLMs, agentic AI, backend engineering, and AI systems.
- I have public TensorFlow open-source contribution work.
- My portfolio is https://minaibrahim.tech/
- I am interested in AI engineering opportunities where I can work on production-focused AI systems.

---

## Draft

Subject: AI Engineering Background — Open to Relevant Opportunities

Hi [Hiring Manager's Name],

I'm reaching out to introduce my background in AI engineering and to ask whether there might be a relevant opportunity on your team.

My work spans Generative AI, LLMs, agentic AI, backend engineering, and AI systems more broadly. I also contribute to TensorFlow's open-source project. You can see examples of this work, including a multi-agent architecture project, in my portfolio: https://minaibrahim.tech/

I'm particularly interested in roles focused on production AI systems, where I can apply this combination of research and engineering experience to real-world problems.

If there's an opportunity that might be a fit, or if it would be useful to talk further, I'd welcome the chance to connect.

Best regards,

[Your Name]

---

## Critique

### Strengths

- Covers all required technical areas.
- Professional and direct.
- Does not invent company-specific information.
- Ends with a soft invitation to connect.

### Problems

- "including a multi-agent architecture project" was not explicitly supplied in this run's facts.
- "research and engineering experience" introduces research even though research was not provided in this run.
- The name placeholders require human completion before sending.

### Required Changes

- Remove details that were carried over from previous context.
- Remove unsupported "research" framing.
- Keep placeholders and explicitly flag them for human completion.
- Preserve the concise tone.
