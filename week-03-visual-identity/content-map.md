# Content Map

## Portfolio Goal

The portfolio should communicate one clear message:

> I design and build Generative AI systems with LLMs, RAG, CAG, agentic workflows, and production-focused backend engineering.

The design should support this message by making projects, technical decisions, and evidence easy to understand.

---

## 1. Home

Purpose:
Give visitors an immediate understanding of who I am and what I build.

Content:
- Name and role
- One-line claim
- Short professional introduction
- Main call to action: View Projects
- Secondary call to action: Contact Me
- A small selection of strongest projects

The homepage should stay simple and should not use decorative visuals that compete with the project work.

---

## 2. Projects

Purpose:
Show proof of my technical work.

Each project card should include:
- Project name
- Problem solved
- Technologies used
- My role
- Short result or outcome
- Real screenshot when available
- Link to case study or repository

Priority project areas:
- Generative AI
- LLM applications
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Cache-Augmented Generation (CAG)
- Agentic AI systems
- NLP
- AI backend services
- APIs and production workflows

---

## 3. Case Studies

Purpose:
Explain selected projects in more depth instead of only showing screenshots.

Each case study should follow:

1. Problem
2. Goal
3. Approach
4. Architecture
5. AI / ML technologies used
6. Engineering decisions
7. Challenges
8. Result
9. Evidence
10. What I learned

Real project screenshots, diagrams, metrics, and repository evidence should be preferred over decorative AI-generated images.

## 5. About

Purpose:
Give enough context about my background without distracting from the work.

Content:
- Short professional background
- AI engineering focus
- Engineering interests
- Current areas of specialization
- Education
- Selected achievements

The section should remain concise.

---

## 6. Contact

Purpose:
Make it easy for recruiters, engineers, and collaborators to reach me.

Content:
- Email
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Optional CV link

No unnecessary forms or visual effects are needed.

---

## Visual Priority

The portfolio should follow this order:

1. Evidence of real work
2. Clear explanations
3. Technical results
4. Consistent visual identity
5. Decorative visuals

Design frames the work; it should never upstage it.
