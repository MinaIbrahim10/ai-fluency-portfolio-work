# Survive the Crit

## Live Portfolio

https://minaibrahim.tech

## Proof Statement

**I can solve complex AI engineering problems in production-grade systems.**

## Review Process

I sent my live portfolio and proof statement to another person and asked them to review the site without me explaining it first.

The first two questions were:

1. In about ten seconds, what do you think I do?
2. Based on the portfolio, would you believe I am good at it? Why or why not?

I then asked for anything confusing, unclear, broken, unsupported, or worth improving.

I collected the feedback without defending the current version.

## Reviewer Feedback

The reviewer said that within the first few seconds they understood that I am an AI engineer with a strong focus on NLP and Generative AI.

They mentioned work involving:

- LLMs
- RAG
- agents
- multi-agent systems
- NLP

For the second question, the reviewer said the portfolio felt strong and believable.

They specifically highlighted my TensorFlow free-threading / GIL work as the most interesting part because it shows both a technical claim and supporting evidence.

The reviewer also said:

- everything was clear
- the UI looked modern and professional
- they did not find anything confusing or broken
- they did not feel the proof statement was unsupported
- they did not identify an urgent change

Their main suggestion was to add stronger direct evidence for:

- Transformer LLM From Scratch
- AI-Powered Journal Management

## Feedback Sort

### Must-Fix

**None identified by the reviewer.**

The reviewer could clearly explain what I do, believed the work supported my technical ability, and did not identify a confusing, broken, or credibility-damaging issue.

I did not create an artificial must-fix just to claim that something had been changed.

### Nice-to-Have

#### Stronger evidence for selected projects

The reviewer suggested adding more direct evidence for Transformer LLM From Scratch and AI-Powered Journal Management.

I do not currently have suitable public source-code repositories for those two projects, so I did not add unsupported or misleading links.

However, during the review I identified that my Multimodal Deepfake Detection project does have a public repository with real implementation evidence.

I updated the live portfolio to link that project directly to:

https://github.com/MinaIbrahim10/deepfake-detection

The repository includes work across text, image, and audio deepfake detection, as well as CNN/OpenCV, LSTM, and FastAPI components.

## Change Made After Review

**Before:**
The Multimodal Deepfake Detection project appeared as a project description without a direct public evidence link.

**After:**
The project now links to its public GitHub repository and is labeled with explicit public repository evidence.

This improved the evidence coverage of the selected-work section without adding unsupported claims.

## Result

The external review confirmed that:

- my AI engineering positioning is understandable quickly
- the Generative AI / NLP / agentic systems focus is clear
- the TensorFlow runtime work provides convincing technical evidence
- the interface is clear and professional
- no must-fix usability or credibility issue was identified

The main improvement area was stronger direct evidence for selected projects, and I addressed this where real public evidence was available.
